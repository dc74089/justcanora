# Rhetorical Arguments

---

## Rhetorical Arguments
> Specific ways of constructing an argument that makes for a compelling (convincing) piece of writing or speech.


----

## Pathos
**Appeal to Emotion**

* "Save the poor, hungry puppy"
* "Come buy this delicious, cheesy, deep dish pizza"
* "Aren't you tired of teachers telling you what to do?"

--- 

## Logos
**Appeal to Logic**

* "Depression is caused by a lack of serotonin in the brain. It has been shown that puppies increase serotonin. Therefore, puppies will cure depression."
* "Doesn't it make sense that..."

---

## Ethos
**Appeal to Credibility**

* "9 out of 10 dentists agree..."
* "Taylor Swift said you should..."
* "According to the CDC..."

----

## Guidelines
* Good arguments should have a balance of Pathos, Logos, and Ethos
* Typically, Pathos is a great intro
* Typically, Logos and Ethos (and Pathos) are great in body paragraphs
* Typically, Logos is great in conclusions
